aim-point-tracker live demo

The tracker's spot detector (aimtrk/, plain C, no heap) compiled to WebAssembly and running on every frame of a simulated 1280 × 800 infrared camera in your browser: background model, noise estimate, threshold, blob table, rejection rules and the sub-pixel centroid. Move the mouse over the camera image to aim the emitter; click to fire a shot. The same C runs in the tracker service and its unit tests. Source

WebAssembly module built from aimtrk/ and web/aimtrk_web.c by scripts/build-web.sh (clang, wasm32, no Emscripten). The frames come from a JavaScript cut-down of the repository's camera simulator (sim/camsim.py): same sensor values, flat screen, no lens. The emitter flashes in every other frame, as a weapon in one of two time slots.
